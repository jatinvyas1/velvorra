Our story

A trade desk built
for the long game.

We started Velvorra International with a simple thesis: India sits at the centre of a once-in-a-generation agro trade opportunity — surplus in some commodities, deficit in others, with rising global demand for quality, traceability and reliability.

Today we move fresh produce, pulses, rice, spices, food ingredients and sugar & oil from Indian origin to buyers across the Gulf, South-East Asia, the EU, the CIS and beyond. Every shipment is sourced from verified origins, inspected before loading and handled with full documentation through delivery at destination.

It’s not glamorous work. It’s spreadsheets, port visits, midnight calls with shipping lines, quality reports and contracts. But done well — consistently, over years — it earns the only thing that really matters in this business: trust.
